retail game Battle Engine Aquila (Lost Toys, 2003) and contributed as this

licence was granted with it, and the project's licence (GPL-3.0-only) is not
claimed for it. It is included only so that the tests can check save handling
against a real save; do not reuse it outside this repository's tests.
